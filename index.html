About

I'm an Applied Scientist at Amazon, where I work on agentic recommendation engines for personalized recommendations and product discovery. I'm also a Visiting Researcher at the University of New South Wales (UNSW), where I work on time-series reasoning using large language models. Previously, I was an Applied Scientist Intern at Amazon, focusing on causal machine learning for customer behavioral analysis. I also interned as a Data Scientist at Meesho, where I developed and productionized computer-vision systems for product grouping and catalog deduplication. Prior to that, I worked as a Research Associate at Mediwhale, where I developed generalizable deep-learning solutions for non-invasive systemic-disease screening from retinal images.

My research interests span foundation models, multiagent systems, multimodal reasoning, and AI for healthcare. Currently, I am working on foundation models for time-series and structured data, time-series language models (TSLMs), reasoning with LLMs, and agentic AI systems for long-form video understanding. My work has been published at top-tier conferences including Interspeech and UbiComp, as well as workshops at ICML, ICLR, AAAI, KDD, ACL, and MICCAI. I've also served as a Program Committee member for MICCAI and workshops at ICML, ICLR, and ACL, and have received the AAAI Student Scholarship and travel grants from KDD, UbiComp, and SIGMOD.

Beyond work, I enjoy traveling, exploring new places, meeting new people, and experiencing different cultures. I love spending time in nature, hiking scenic trails, and capturing those moments through photography. In my free time, I enjoy reading books and poetry, learning new languages, and exploring new ideas. Whether it's AI, travel, books, or simply sharing ideas, I'm always happy to connect — feel free to reach out!
