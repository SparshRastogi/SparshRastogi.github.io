1. Scope and application

This privacy notice (the “Notice”) describes the processing of personal data carried out in connection with the website published at sparshrastogi.github.io and its subordinate resources (together, the “Site”). It is issued in satisfaction of the information obligations arising under Articles 13 and 14 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (the “GDPR”), the corresponding provisions of the United Kingdom General Data Protection Regulation, and such other data protection or privacy legislation as may apply to a given data subject by reason of residence.

The Notice applies exclusively to processing undertaken by or on behalf of the controller identified in section 3. It does not extend to any third-party website, repository, publisher platform or preprint archive to which the Site links, each of which operates under its own privacy arrangements, and the controller accepts no responsibility for the practices of any such destination.

Where any provision of this Notice is held to be invalid or unenforceable in a given jurisdiction, the remaining provisions continue in full force.

2. Definitions

Terms used in this Notice bear the meanings given to them in Article 4 GDPR. For convenience:

  • “Personal data” means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.
  • “Processing” means any operation performed on personal data, whether or not by automated means, including collection, recording, storage, consultation, use, disclosure and erasure.
  • “Controller” means the person who determines the purposes and means of processing; here, the person identified in section 3.
  • “Processor” means a person who processes personal data on behalf of the controller; here, the parties identified in section 11.
  • “Data subject” means the natural person to whom personal data relates; here, a visitor to the Site.
  • “Terminal equipment” means the device by which the data subject accesses the Site.

3. Identity and contact details of the controller

The controller is Sparsh Rastogi, an individual, contactable for all matters arising under this Notice at sparshrastogi03@gmail.com. The Site is maintained in a personal and non-commercial capacity for the purpose of publishing academic and professional work. No data protection officer has been designated, no such designation being required under Article 37 GDPR in respect of processing of this nature and scale.

4. Categories of personal data processed

The following categories of personal data are processed automatically upon and during access to the Site:

  • Technical and connection data — user-agent string; browser and rendering engine; operating system; device classification; screen and viewport dimensions; language preference; and approximate geolocation resolved to country and region level only.
  • Usage data — resources requested; the date, time and duration of engagement; the referring uniform resource locator, together with any campaign or attribution parameters appended to it; and the sequence of navigation within the Site.
  • Interaction data — events arising from interaction with the interface, comprising pointer and touch input, scroll position and depth, activation of controls, and the identity of outbound hyperlinks and document downloads selected. Such data are captured both by generalised instrumentation and by discrete named events defined by the controller, including the selection of a publication record together with its ordinal position within the list as then ordered, the selection of a curriculum vitae document, and the sections of the Site reached during a visit.
  • Session replay data — the Site employs session replay technology, being instrumentation which records the interaction described in the preceding paragraph in a form permitting a reconstruction of the visit to be rendered and subsequently reviewed by the controller. Text entered into form fields is masked at source and is not transmitted.
  • Preference data — the elections described in section 9, retained on the terminal equipment and not transmitted.

No name, postal address, telephone number, electronic mail address, account credential, payment instrument or other directly identifying particular is requested or processed by the Site. No special categories of personal data within the meaning of Article 9 GDPR, and no personal data relating to criminal convictions or offences within the meaning of Article 10 GDPR, are knowingly processed.

5. Sources of the data

All personal data described in section 4 are obtained directly from the data subject’s terminal equipment at the time of access, whether transmitted automatically by the browser in the ordinary course of making a request or generated by interaction with the interface. No personal data are obtained from any third-party source, data broker, enrichment service or publicly available register, and no attempt is made to combine the data described in this Notice with any other dataset.

6. Instrumentation and means of processing

Processing is effected by a client-side script served from the processor's distribution endpoint and executed within the browser of the data subject. Transmission to the processor is made over Transport Layer Security to the endpoint us.i.posthog.com, events being despatched in batches during the visit and upon its conclusion. The instrumentation is initialised with a public, write-only project key, which is necessarily disclosed in the source of the Site and which confers no capacity to read, export or otherwise obtain access to data already held.

The following measures of data minimisation are applied at source, that is to say before any transmission occurs:

  • text entered into any input, textarea or editable element is masked and is not transmitted;
  • geolocation is resolved to country and region only, no finer resolution being requested or retained;
  • no fingerprinting technique is employed beyond those ordinary characteristics which the browser discloses in the course of making a request;
  • no identifier is shared with, or reconciled against, any other website, and no cross-site or cross-device tracking is performed;
  • where an automated "Do Not Track" signal is present the instrumentation is not initialised and no processing occurs.

9. Purposes of processing and legal basis

Processing is undertaken for the following purposes: to measure the reach and audience of published academic work; to ascertain which materials and records are consulted, and in what sequence; to diagnose defects in presentation and navigation across the range of devices in use; and to inform the maintenance and improvement of the Site.

The legal basis for such processing is the legitimate interests pursued by the controller pursuant to Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, being an interest in understanding the reach and utility of published work. The controller has considered the interests and fundamental rights of data subjects and has determined that they do not override that interest, having regard in particular to the following: the data are pseudonymous and are not used to identify any individual; no special category data are processed; the data are not used for advertising, are not sold, and are not disclosed otherwise than to the processors identified in section 11; and an unconditional means of objection is provided by section 15.

In so far as the storing of information on, or the gaining of access to information already stored in, the terminal equipment of the data subject by non-essential means requires consent under Article 5(3) of Directive 2002/58/EC as transposed, or under any equivalent provision, such consent constitutes the legal basis for the operations concerned, and is obtained and may be withdrawn as described in section 15.

Data transmitted to the processor identified in section 11 are secured in transit and are subject to the technical and organisational measures maintained by that processor, particulars of which are published by it.

Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

  • An automated “Do Not Track” signal transmitted by the browser is honoured. Where such a signal is present, no analytics processing is initiated and the notice referred to above is not presented.
  • Any content-blocking extension or equivalent facility will likewise prevent processing. No function of the Site is contingent upon analytics operating, and no content is withheld from a data subject who declines.

  • Erasure (Article 17) — to obtain the deletion of personal data where one of the grounds specified in that Article applies.
  • Restriction (Article 18) — to obtain the restriction of processing in the circumstances specified in that Article.
  • Portability (Article 20) — to receive personal data in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format, in so far as the conditions of that Article are satisfied.
  • Objection (Article 21) — to object at any time, on grounds relating to the particular situation of the data subject, to processing based upon legitimate interests. The control provided in section 15 gives effect to such an objection immediately and without the need for any justification.

Requests may be addressed to the contact given in section 3 and will be responded to without undue delay and in any event within one month of receipt, save where an extension is permitted. No fee is charged unless a request is manifestly unfounded or excessive.

The processing described in this Notice is pseudonymous and not anonymous. It is carried out against a device identifier which persists between visits, with the consequence that the data subject remains identifiable within the meaning of Recital 26 GDPR notwithstanding that no name or contact particular is held. The controller holds no means of associating that identifier with a named individual, and may therefore be unable to identify the data subject within the meaning of Article 11(1) GDPR upon a request unaccompanied by further particulars.

Article 11(2) preserves the rights conferred by Articles 15 to 20 where the data subject supplies information enabling identification. Such information is available to the data subject directly: the identifier is stored upon the terminal equipment and may be disclosed by means of the control below. A request quoting that identifier will be actioned against the corresponding records held by the processor, and erasure will be effected in respect of them.

Clearing site data in the browser discards that identifier and prevents any future association of the terminal equipment with records already held. It does not, of itself, erase those records, and is accordingly not a substitute for a request made under this section.
